# fleeting/ — Quick Captures

Raw, unpolished ideas. Created during CAPTURE operation or as the first step of INGEST when owner drops an idea without a full source.

## Filename

`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md` — date-prefixed to track when the idea surfaced.

## Required Frontmatter

```yaml
---
title: "Quick Idea"
type: fleeting
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [domain]
---
```

## Content Guidelines

- 1–5 sentences. Do not over-polish.
- Raw is fine — the point is capture, not completeness.
- Add a `[[wikilink]]` if an obvious existing permanent note connects to this idea.

## Lifecycle

Fleeting notes are **temporary by design**. They either:

- Get promoted to a permanent note (owner decides)
- Get discarded (owner decides)
- Stay as-is if the idea doesn't grow

Do not auto-promote fleeting notes to permanent without owner asking.
