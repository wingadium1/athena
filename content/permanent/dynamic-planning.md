Dynamic planning is the ability of an agent to adapt its [[planning-loop|plan]] during execution based on real-time observations from its environment. This allows for greater adaptability but introduces unpredictability.

**Why it matters**: Dynamic planning is essential for agents operating in unpredictable environments or tackling open-ended goals. However, it requires strong monitoring and fallback mechanisms to prevent issues like goal drift.
