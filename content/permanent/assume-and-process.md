Assume and Process is a strategy for handling ambiguity where an agent makes a reasonable inference about an ambiguous goal and continues execution without waiting for human clarification.

**When to use**: This strategy is suitable for low-stakes, easily reversible, or time-sensitive tasks where the cost of a wrong assumption is minimal and the context is well-constrained. The goal is appropriate judgment, not maximum caution.
