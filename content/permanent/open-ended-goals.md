Open-ended goals are objectives where the target is known, but the exact steps to achieve it cannot be fully pre-specified at design time.

**Why it matters**: The presence of open-ended goals is a key signal that agentic complexity may be warranted, as agents can dynamically plan and adapt their approach to achieve the goal.
