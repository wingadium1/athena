Reasoning Errors occur when the agentic system generates an incorrect plan, selects an inappropriate tool, or misinterprets provided information despite having adequate input. These errors are internal to the model.

## Recovery
These errors typically cannot be resolved through retries. Recovery requires modifications to the prompt, refinement of the context window, or escalation to human review/intervention.
