Semantic Validation assesses whether agentic outputs make sense within their specific context, extending beyond simple structural or schema compliance.

## Goal
To catch errors that structural checks miss—such as illogical date formats, inconsistent data across fields, or implausible values that pass structural schemas but are semantically incorrect.
