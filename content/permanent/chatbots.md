Chatbots are systems that can generate human-like text responses in a conversation. However, they are fundamentally stateless and cannot take actions in the real world.

**Why it matters**: Understanding the limitations of chatbots is crucial for knowing when to transition to a more capable agentic system that can perform actions via tool use.
