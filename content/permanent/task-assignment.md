Task Assignment is a core function of an orchestrator agent. It involves routing and matching subtasks to the appropriate narrow-scope sub-agents that have the capabilities to execute them.

**Why it matters**: Efficient task assignment ensures that the right agent is working on the right task, leading to more effective and reliable execution.
