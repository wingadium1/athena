Hybrid topologies in multi-agent orchestration combine different patterns (e.g., Hub-and-Spoke, Pipeline, Peer-to-Peer) to leverage the strengths of each.

**Why it matters**: Real-world systems often require a mix of topologies. Careful design of the boundaries and handoffs between the different topological patterns is crucial for managing state and ensuring reliability.
