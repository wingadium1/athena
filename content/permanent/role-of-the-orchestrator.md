The Role of the Orchestrator is to manage tasks and synthesize results in a workflow or agentic system. It decompresses a high-level goal, assigns subtasks to sub-agents or tools, and synthesizes the final results.

**Why it matters**: The orchestrator is a crucial component for coordinating complex work, ensuring that all parts of the system work together to achieve the overall goal.
