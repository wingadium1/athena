The Clarify First strategy is a cautious approach to handling ambiguity where an agent pauses its execution to seek more information from a human before proceeding.

**When to use**: This strategy is essential for high-stakes, irreversible, or high-cost operations, or when the scope of the task is uncertain. The added latency of clarification is a worthwhile trade-off to prevent costly mistakes.
